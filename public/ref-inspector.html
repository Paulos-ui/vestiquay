How it works
The inspector calls get_deposits on the exchange through NEAR RPC. It displays positive balances in the integer units returned by each token, alongside the block height. An empty result applies only to this exchange.
The public account is sent to the RPC provider. The client needs no wallet connection or signing key for inspection. Live withdrawal signing and code-version verification are planned milestones.
